# Bundled Bible text

World English Bible Catholic Edition (WEBC) — modern English, public domain, in traditional Catholic book order. Source: eBible.org eng-web-c VPL text (https://ebible.org/Scriptures/eng-web-c_vpl.zip). The World English Bible text is dedicated to the public domain by eBible.org; "World English Bible" is a trademark of eBible.org for unchanged copies, so the text is bundled byte-for-byte as published. Complete Catholic canon: 73 books, including Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach (Ecclesiasticus), Baruch, and 1-2 Maccabees, plus the longer Greek portions of Esther and Daniel. The New American Bible hosted on vatican.va (snapshot 2002-11-11; revised as NABRE 2011) is © Confraternity of Christian Doctrine / USCCB and cannot be bundled without written permission, so it is linked, not copied.
