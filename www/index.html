BibleVerse

Every verse has a name.

A Christian companion for daily Scripture. Pray with short named verses in the Daily Feed, open the complete Catholic Bible any time, and carry it all with you — no account, no server, no connection needed.

  • Daily FeedNamed verses to pray with — save, reflect, and share. Double-tap a card to keep it close.
  • Feasts & SaintsEvery Catholic holy day and saint day, with the liturgical season — browse the calendar or see what's next.
  • Full Bible, offlineAll 73 books of the Catholic Bible in modern English (World English Bible Catholic Edition, public domain) live inside the app — browse, search, copy and share any verse.
  • Private by designLikes, saves and your reading place stay on this device only.

Bible text: World English Bible Catholic Edition (WEBC) — modern English, public domain, in traditional Catholic book order with the deuterocanonical books and the Greek portions of Esther and Daniel. The New American Bible on vatican.va is copyrighted and can't ship inside the app, so the app bundles this public-domain Catholic edition instead.

Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026 · Version 1.0.0

The short version: BibleVerse collects nothing. No account, no server, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your device.

  • No data collectedWe collect, transmit, sell, and share nothing — no personal data, no usage data, no diagnostics. The full Bible ships inside the app, so there is nothing to send home to.
  • On your device onlyLikes, saves, comments, your reading position, and text-size preference live in private on-device storage. Deleting the app removes them permanently.
  • Sharing is yoursShare opens your device's share sheet or copies text. What you share goes only where you send it — we receive and store nothing.
  • ChildrenWe collect no data from anyone, including children.
  • ContactQuestions: support@bibleverse.app. The public policy is at https://bibleverse-bg9.pages.dev/privacy. Help is at https://bibleverse-bg9.pages.dev/support.
