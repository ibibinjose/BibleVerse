Support

BibleVerse 1.0.0 · No account needed

BibleVerse is a daily Scripture companion with the full Catholic Bible inside the app. It works offline and does not have accounts, so there is nothing to reset or sign in to.

Contact

Email support@bibleverse.app. Include the device (iPhone or Android and the OS version), the app version (Profile, at the bottom), and what you expected to happen.

Common questions

  • Where is my data? Likes, saves, comments, and your reading place stay on this device only. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, removes them.
  • Does it need a connection? No. The Bible text is bundled in the app. On the web, a service worker caches the same files after the first visit.
  • Which Bible is this? World English Bible Catholic Edition (public domain), 73 books, modern English.
  • Privacy The app collects no data. Read the privacy policy.
