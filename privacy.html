Privacy Policy

Effective: November 12, 2026 · App version 1.0.0

The short version: BibleVerse collects nothing. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Everything you do in the app stays on your own device.

1. Data we collect

None. BibleVerse does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, usage data, or diagnostics with us or with any third party. The app works fully offline — the complete Bible text ships inside the app, so there is nothing to send home to.

2. Data stored on your device (never leaves it)

The following stays in private on-device storage and is never transmitted:

  • Likes, saves, and comments on verse cards
  • Your Bible reading position (“Continue reading”)
  • Reader text-size preference

Deleting the app — or clearing its data in your device settings — permanently removes all of the above.

3. Sharing

When you tap Share, the app opens your device’s standard share sheet (or copies text to the clipboard). Whatever you choose to share goes only to the app or person you select. BibleVerse itself receives and stores nothing from this.

4. Network access

The iOS and Android apps make no network requests of their own. On Android the app declares the standard INTERNET permission, which is required by the WebView container that displays the app — the app uses it to load its own bundled files, not to contact any server.

5. Children

BibleVerse collects no data from anyone, including children. The content is a Christian Bible suitable for all ages.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will ship inside the app and be posted at this same address before the change takes effect.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@bibleverse.app. For other help, see Support.
